FAQ

Questions you might have.

A few answers before you decide whether therapy is right for you.

What happens in the first session? +

The first session is mainly about understanding what you're going through, what brought you here, and what you'd like to work on.

Do I need to know exactly what's wrong? +

No. You don't need to have everything figured out before starting. Understanding what is happening is part of the process.

Is therapy confidential? +

Sessions are intended to provide a private and confidential space where you can speak openly and honestly.

How long is a session? +

Session length and frequency can be discussed when booking, depending on your needs and circumstances.

Can I book an online session? +

Online sessions can be arranged. Send a message through WhatsApp to discuss availability and the suitable format.
